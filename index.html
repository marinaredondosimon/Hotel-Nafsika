Bar & Restaurant

Bar One &
The Taverna

At Nafsika Hotel we want to make sure you have everything you need for a comfortable and enjoyable holiday. Our facilities are designed with your needs in mind so you can fully enjoy your Greek experience.

We serve authentic Greek cuisine made with love and tradition, using Theodora's recipes passed down through generations — from savory souvlaki to our famous moussaka.

Our Story

The heart
of Nafsika

The Nafsika Hotel's main restaurant — we call it a taverna! — is open to everyone and offers waiter-served continental breakfast, daytime Greek dishes and snacks, and homemade Mediterranean and international dinners.

Enjoy your dinner with the cool breeze of the Ionian Sea and clap along to the impromptu Greek dancing by Speros and the team. A family tradition that makes every evening at Nafsika truly unforgettable.
