Bar One

Relax, sip, and enjoy the view

The Bar

Overlooking the
Ionian Sea

Bar One is the perfect place to unwind with refreshing cocktails and good company. Whether you want to watch the sunset with a glass of something cold, play billiards with friends, or simply relax by the pool — Bar One has you covered.

Come and enjoy with us. Throughout the season we host live music evenings, traditional Greek dance nights, and fun activities and games for children, creating memorable experiences for guests of all ages.
